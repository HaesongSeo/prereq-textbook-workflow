### How to read this PDF

> For a scan, always record this. At minimum these three:
>
> - **Can the text layer be trusted?** If not, record how it breaks, with an
>   example (what OCR does to subscripts and integral signs).
> - **The page-image render command.**
>   `pdftoppm -r 150 -f <PDF page> -l <PDF page> -png "<file>" /tmp/out`
> - **Page correspondence.** Journal page vs PDF page (the cover throws it off).
>
> **Even when the text layer looks intact**, things like the underline of
> $\liminf$ are lost. Symbols a statement's correctness hangs on are confirmed
> from the image.
